Our story

It started with Lilian's idea.

MOCLLA is a family business created by the Orji Akanu family while living in Geneva. Our 7-year-old daughter, Lilian, came to us with an idea: a family business that sells everything for families. She named it MOCLLA — the initials of each member of our family and our surname, in no particular order. We decided to support her.

Lilian also designed our logo, first drawn with pencils and upgraded today: a family wrapped together, with a star for dreams and a heart for love.

We are bringing innovation to family lifestyle, beginning with fashion — creating trend-conscious, intentional families.

Questions

How do I order?

Add shoes to your bag and press “Send order on WhatsApp”, or message us directly on +234 703 511 2001 or +234 806 802 8794. Your order arrives in our chat; we confirm stock, delivery and payment details with you there.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is free when you buy 2 pairs or more. For a single pair we confirm the delivery fee on WhatsApp before you pay.

Which sizes do you have?

EU sizes 26 to 39, depending on the style. Each style shows its sizes. Not sure? Send us your child's foot length in cm and we'll help.

Can I return shoes for a refund?

Our 30-day promise is an exchange for another size or style. If a pair is faulty, tell us and we'll make it right.

What does the name MOCLLA mean?

It's made from the initials of each member of our family and our surname, chosen by Lilian, age 7.
